Research Interests

Research Interests

Field
Econometrics

My research focuses on the estimation of spatial weight matrices and the development of econometric methods for addressing endogeneity.

I am more interested in addressing practical challenges faced by empirical researchers than in developing new methods or computational techniques for their own sake. I am also interested in understanding the restrictions imposed by assumptions commonly made in empirical analysis and exploring what happens when these assumptions are relaxed.

My research also draws on Bayesian methods where appropriate. Rather than committing to a particular methodological framework, I believe in choosing methods that best suit the problem at hand.

I believe that econometrics is most valuable when it can be put into practice. My aim is to pursue questions that I find intellectually interesting while developing methods that are useful to empirical researchers.
